Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 May 2026

Who We Are

xwall provides ambient wallpapers and optional music playlist features.

Information We Collect

When you sign in, we may receive basic profile information from your provider, such as your name, email address, and profile image.

We also store app usage and playlist preferences needed to provide core features.

How We Use Information

We use your information to authenticate your account, show your saved playlists, and improve app reliability and security.

Third-Party Services

xwall integrates with Google/YouTube and Spotify for authentication and playlist access. Your use of those services is also governed by their own policies.

Data Retention

We keep account-related data only as long as needed to operate the service or as required by law.

Your Choices

You can sign out at any time. You can request deletion of your account data by contacting the support email listed on the OAuth consent screen.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us via the support email shown in our OAuth consent details.
